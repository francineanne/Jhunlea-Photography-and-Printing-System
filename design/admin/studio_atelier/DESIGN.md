## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the craft, precision, and modern editorial presence of a premium photography studio and fine art print laboratory. It balances the timeless tactile heritage of photographic printmaking with the slick, confident polish of contemporary digital platforms.

### Personality & Tone
- **Editorial & Tactile:** High-end gallery aesthetics, expansive white space, structured framing, and disciplined alignment.
- **Warm Authority:** Professional, reassuring, and rooted in physical craftsmanship.
- **Modern Filipino Craftsmanship:** Premium local atelier energy that rejects cluttered mass-retail noise in favor of curated clarity.

### Design Movement
**Minimal Editorial Gallery:** Driven by deliberate negative space, razor-thin borders (`1px solid #E5E5E5`), stark monochromatic contrasts, and surgical crimson focal accents (`#C62828`). The UI acts as a bespoke mount board or framing mat, elevating client imagery and print deliverables without competing for visual dominance.

## Colors

The palette adopts an editorial gallery approach where ink-black and crimson anchor the hierarchy against pristine photographic paper tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#C62828`):** Deep crimson red reserved for pivotal actions, active navigation pips, badge highlights, and essential states. Primary hover drops to dark crimson (`#B71C1C`).
- **Dark Neutral (`#171717`):** Neutral dark ink for primary text, solid iconography, high-contrast headings, and dark button variants.
- **Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure gallery wall white.
- **Surface Light (`#FBFBFB`):** Subtle backdrops and secondary sections.
- **Surface Card (`#F5F5F5`):** Structural card fills, grouped input backgrounds, and framing enclosures.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5E5E5`):** 1px delineators separating layers, photo slots, and table rows.
- **Text Secondary (`#525252`):** Body copy, metadata labels, and instructions.
- **Text Muted (`#A3A3A3`):** Placeholders, subtle timestamps, and deactivated states.

### Status Tones (Pills & Indicators)
- **Amber (`#D97706` text on `#FEF3C7` background):** Under review, pending verification.
- **Blue (`#2563EB` text on `#EFF6FF` background):** Awaiting schedule, booking slot reserved.
- **Violet (`#7C3AED` text on `#F5F3FF` background):** Processing in darkroom/print laboratory.
- **Green (`#16A34A` text on `#DCFCE7` background):** Ready for pickup, booking confirmed, settled.
- **Gray (`#4B5563` text on `#F3F4F6` background):** Completed archive, cancelled request.
- **Red (`#DC2626` text on `#FEE2E2` background):** Rejected upload, unpaid balance, alert.

## Typography

The typography hierarchy uses three disciplined roles:

1. **Playfair Display:** Elevates storytelling, studio session titles, marketing headlines, and gallery banners with refined, literary contrast.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives functional navigation, body narratives, form labels, tooltips, and operational microcopy with geometric legibility.
3. **JetBrains Mono:** Manages technical specifications, photo dimensions (`8x10"`, `300 DPI`), order identifiers (`PR-0012`), camera EXIF parameters, and invoice line items.

### Content & Financial Rules
- Format all currency with the official Philippine Peso sign: **₱** (e.g., `₱1,250.00`).
- Numbers in price tokens must use tabular numeric alignments (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Max Container
- All public catalog, booking, and studio interfaces align to a maximum container width of **1280px** centered horizontally.
- Primary layout operates on a 12-column grid on desktop (`1024px+`) with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
- Tablet devices (`768px - 1023px`) use an 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- Mobile screens (`< 768px`) collapse to a 4-column flow with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` page edge margins.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **space-xs (4px):** Micro gaps between icons and labels, badge internal vertical padding.
- **space-sm (8px):** Form field inner gap, pill badge padding, card header subtext gap.
- **space-md (16px):** Standard input padding, card interior layout gaps, list item spacing.
- **space-lg (24px):** Card exterior margins, section group padding, studio service block separation.
- **space-xl (40px):** Major vertical section dividers, gallery category row gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, instead using crisp borders and faint, warm-tinted diffused shadows reminiscent of natural daylight falling onto studio prints.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Default `#FFFFFF` or `#FBFBFB` canvas bounded by `1px solid #E5E5E5`.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Faint ambient lift (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.02)`) with a bounding border of `1px solid #E5E5E5`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Lift):** Elevated focus state (`0 12px 24px -6px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Deep photographic staging depth (`0 24px 48px -12px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.16)`).

### Motion & Micro-interactions
- **Card Hover:** Combined `translateY(-3px)` card lift accompanied by a subtle zoom (`scale(1.03)`) on contained photo thumbnails over `280ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Button Sweep:** Primary crimson CTA buttons feature a radial or linear dark crimson (`#B71C1C`) fill sweep that originates from the click origin on engagement.
- **Loading Aperture:** Asynchronous requests trigger a rotating camera iris / aperture spinner icon instead of standard circular progress bars.

## Shapes

The geometry strikes a balance between architectural precision and modern touch accessibility:

- **Cards & Frame Panels:** Exact `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), mirroring rounded archival photo mounts.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Select Controls:** Exact `10px` border radius.
- **Pill Badges, Avatars & Status Indicators:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create distinction between content surfaces and interactive status tags.
- **Image Frames:** `8px` or `12px` with clipping masks (`overflow: hidden`) to preserve structural edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#C62828`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `10px`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (14px). Hover state transitions to `#B71C1C`. Features subtle inner shadow on active click.
- **Secondary / Studio Dark:** Background `#171717`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `10px`. Hover transitions to `#262626`.
- **Outline / Neutral:** Background `transparent`, text `#171717`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, hover background `#F5F5F5`.
- **Dimensions:** Default height `44px` (padding: `0 20px`), compact height `36px` (padding: `0 14px`).

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Background `#F5F5F5` or `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, corner radius `12px`.
- Padding: `24px` on desktop, `16px` on mobile.
- Hoverable cards apply Level 2 elevation and image frame expansion.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, border-radius `10px`, padding `0 14px`.
- Text color `#171717`, placeholder `#A3A3A3`.
- Focus state: border color `#C62828` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.12)`.

### Status Pills
- Pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `4px 12px`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 12px bold.
- Includes a 6px solid status dot matching the text tone.
- Variants: Amber (`#D97706`), Blue (`#2563EB`), Violet (`#7C3AED`), Green (`#16A34A`), Gray (`#4B5563`), Red (`#DC2626`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #E5E5E5`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio).
- Checked state: background `#C62828`, border `#C62828`, animated stroke checkmark in white (`#FFFFFF`).

### Lists & Order Rows
- Monospace reference codes (`JetBrains Mono`, e.g., `PR-0012`) aligned left or metadata stacked.
- Divider: hairline separator `1px solid #E5E5E5`.

### Studio-Specific Notice Banner (Mandatory Transaction Policy)
- Permanent informational badge displayed during checkout, booking steps, and order summaries:
  - Container: Background `#F5F5F5`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, left accent bar `4px solid #C62828`, border-radius `10px`, padding `16px`.
  - Icon: Cash / studio storefront glyph in `#C62828`.
  - Text: *"Payment is made face-to-face at Jhunlea Photography & Printing. No online checkout."*
