Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our reservation policy, rush turnaround, and in-store pick-up.

No online checkout is required. Under our studio policy, payment is made 100% face-to-face at Jhunlea Photography & Printing. You can book your slot or upload files online, receive an owner confirmation, inspect your prints or proofs in-store, and settle conveniently at the counter via cash or QR PH.

Rush ID photos are typically shot, retouched with basic skin tone balancing, printed, and cut in 10 to 15 minutes. Walk-ins are always welcomed Monday through Saturday with no advance booking required.

Absolutely! We encourage clients to bring milestone accessories, musical instruments, floral props, and clean pets. Please notify us during your online reservation so our studio crew can prepare pet-friendly flooring mats and appropriate lighting diffusers.

We stock an extensive paper archive in-house: 260gsm Ultra Lustre, High Gloss, Deep Velvet Matte, and 310gsm Museum-grade Fine Art 100% Cotton Rag. We also offer stretched matte canvas, archival dry-mounting, and minimalist wooden frames.
