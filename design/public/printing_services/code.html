Can I bring my own special paper or vellum stock for printing? expand_more

Yes. We accommodate customer-supplied archival fine art media and parchment, provided it is compatible with either dry toner laser or aqueous pigment inkjet printers. Our technicians will conduct a 1-sheet calibration test before executing the entire print run.

How fast can rush thesis hardbinding and foil stamping be completed? expand_more

Standard hardbinding turnaround is 24 to 48 hours to ensure bookbinding glues properly cure under heavy hydraulic presses. For graduation deadlines, we offer an expedited same-day rush service (submission before 11:00 AM, ready for 5:30 PM counter pickup) subject to rush queue availability.

How do I transmit large print-ready files (above 100MB)? expand_more

You may upload heavy PSD, TIFF, or print-ready ZIP archives via Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer and include the shared link when requesting an online quotation. Alternatively, clients are welcome to drop off files via USB flash drive directly at our Poblacion studio desk.

When and how do I pay for my print order? expand_more

Per studio policy, all payments are conducted face-to-face at the Jhunlea Photography & Printing atelier counter. We accept Cash, GCash QR, Maya, and major Philippine debit/credit cards upon physical job collection.
