Frequently Asked Questions & Studio Guidelines

Can my parents or siblings join me for the session? expand_more
Yes! The Graduation Prestige Package generously includes up to two (2) family members or companions at no added charge for the final portrait set. Please ensure guests arrive dressed and photo-ready by minute 40 of your booking.
Do you provide graduation togas, hoods, and academic caps? expand_more
We maintain a pristine in-house wardrobe of black basic academic togas, mortarboards, and common collegiate hoods (UP, UST, DLSU, Ateneo, FEU, and standard golden tassels). However, for unique departmental hood colors, we recommend bringing your institution's specific regalia.
How and when do I choose which photos get printed and retouched? expand_more
Immediately following your shoot, our photographer will sit down with you at our 32-inch calibrated grading monitor to flag your preferred top shots. Alternatively, an online proofing link can be sent to your email within 24 hours so you can choose at home.
What is the physical print collection timeline? expand_more
Once you approve your master retouched proof, our darkroom and framing workshop crafts your 8R archival framed print and 8 wallet copies in 3 to 5 business days. You can pick them up directly at our studio desk or book courier pickup (Grab/Lalamove).
